# Code of conduct

Agent HUD contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful and
productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code with concrete evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy: redact local paths, configuration, prompts and credentials.
- Accept that maintainers may reject changes that break host contracts,
  accessibility, security or the inherited HUD design rationale.
- Help de-escalate misunderstandings and assume good faith while evidence is
  gathered.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publishing private correspondence, and sustained
disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or moderation channel. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid
conflicts of interest and may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict
participation or ban contributors when necessary.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, reviews, community
spaces and public representation of the project.
